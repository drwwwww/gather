# Hiring outreach help

Who to hire to reach churches for Gather, what to ask for, where to look, and how to pay them. Hand new hires the scripts in [church-outreach-playbook.md](church-outreach-playbook.md) (sections 4–7) and give each one a referral code (section 3).

## People who visit churches in person

Ask for: **"Field sales representative" / "Outside sales rep" / "Brand ambassador (B2B)"**, ideally someone **who is part of a local church** and comfortable talking to pastors. Retired pastors, church administrators, ministry students, seminary students and youth pastors who want part-time work are ideal: they speak the language and are trusted on sight.

Where to post: Indeed, Craigslist (gigs), local seminary and Christian college job boards, your own church's bulletin, LinkedIn, ministerial association newsletters.

Job post title: *Part-time Church Outreach Representative (commission + base)*

What to ask for in the post:
- Part of a local church; comfortable visiting church offices and talking with pastors and administrators.
- Reliable car; available weekdays 10am–3pm, 10–15 hours a week.
- Organized: logs every visit in a shared Google Sheet the same day.
- Comfortable with a phone demo (you train them; it's three screens).
- Bonus: church admin, worship team, or volunteer-coordination experience.

What to ask in the interview:
1. "Walk me through how your church schedules volunteers." (Do they understand the pain?)
2. "Pitch me something you love in 60 seconds." (Can they talk naturally?)
3. "A pastor says 'we're happy with what we have.' What do you say?" (Do they listen or push?)
4. Role-play the front-desk opener from section 4.

How to pay (suggested, adjust to your budget):
- **$75–150 per paid church** (roughly one month of their plan), paid after the church's first payment clears and the 14-day guarantee window passes.
- **$15–25 per completed setup session / trial started** with a real church, so they're not working purely on commission early on.
- Optional small hourly base ($12–15/hr) for the first month while they learn.
- Treat them as **1099 contractors**; have them sign a simple agreement (commission terms, what happens on refunds, no promising features that don't exist).

## Cold callers and cold emailers (Upwork / Fiverr)

**Upwork search terms:**
- `B2B appointment setter`
- `cold calling appointment setting` (filter: US-based, English native, 90%+ job success)
- `SDR` / `sales development representative`
- `cold email specialist Instantly` (or `Smartlead`, `Lemlist`): someone who sets up the domain, warm-up and sequences
- `lead generation nonprofit` / `church outreach`: people who've done this niche
- `virtual assistant lead list building` for building the church list from Google Maps

**Fiverr search terms:**
- `cold email campaign setup` (domain + warm-up + sequence)
- `B2B lead list` / `targeted email list building` (ask for churches by ZIP code, with staff names and roles, verified)
- `appointment setting cold calling`

What to put in the brief:
- Target: US churches of 75–300 people (or student organizations) within {area}; contact = church administrator, executive pastor or worship director.
- Goal: **booked 20-minute demos on your calendar** (give them a Calendly / Google booking link), not "leads."
- Script: sections 5 and 6 above, plus the objection table.
- Pay: per **qualified demo that actually happens** (typical $30–75 each), or hourly ($10–20/hr offshore, $20–35/hr US) with a weekly minimum of dials. Avoid pure "per lead" deals; they produce junk.
- Rules: office lines only, no texting scraped cell numbers, CAN-SPAM footer on every email, never claim features we don't have, and every contact goes into the shared sheet.
- Start with a **paid trial of 1–2 weeks** with 2–3 freelancers, keep the one with the most demos that showed up.

## Before they start

- Give each person their `?ref=CODE` links and a QR leave-behind card.
- Share the Google Sheet and the offer numbers (discount codes, founding-church spots).
- Role-play the door opener and the objection table with them once.
- Pay only on the outcome that matters: demos that happened, setups done, churches that paid and stayed past the 14-day guarantee.
