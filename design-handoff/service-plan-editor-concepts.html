Service plan editor: three directions

Today the editor is two long lists of identical dropdown rows (18 roles and 11 steps), with a status dropdown nobody needs to touch and an alphabetical people list with no context. These are mockups only; nothing is built.

A. Service board Recommended

One screen, two columns. The order of service on the left gets real clock times (9:00, 9:05…) computed from the start time. The team on the right is grouped by ministry with coverage at a glance. People are avatars whose ring shows their answer (green confirmed, amber waiting, red declined), with a small badge for the backup. Status is never a dropdown: it comes from the volunteer. A sticky bar keeps readiness and the two big actions in view.

The people picker is the real win: it opens from any "+" or avatar. Suggestions come first, with their reason; away and double-booked people are shown but greyed out with why. After you pick someone, the same popover offers "Add a backup?", so the backup is one step instead of a second dropdown on every row.

B. Timeline

The service drawn as time. Each ministry is a lane and each step is a block sized by its length, so overruns and idle volunteers are obvious. Great for worship and tech directors planning transitions; weaker for quickly filling roles. It could be a second view of Concept A rather than the main editor.

Small wins that fit any direction

Real clock times

Show 9:05, 9:08… next to each step and "ends 10:33" in the header, instead of offsets like "8m".

Drop the status dropdown

Show status as the avatar ring. Admins only need "Mark confirmed" occasionally, so it moves to the avatar's menu.

Group roles by ministry

Worship / Tech / Hospitality / Kids, with "3 / 5" coverage per group. Unused roles stay hidden as they are today.

Keyboard

N new step, ⌘↑/↓ move step, A assign the focused role, ⌘S save now.

Preview as volunteer

A toggle that shows exactly what the mobile bulletin will look like, so admins trust what they publish.

One home for scheduling

Volunteers, Service Plans and the plan editor all edit the same data. Point Volunteers' "edit" at the board so there's one place to learn.
